rhY uses Brave (~5 GB) while Strata runs and said plainly they won't close it (2026-10-31). Treat it as a fixed cost.

**Why:** it's their working browser; Strata has to fit around their desktop, not the other way round.
**How to apply:** never suggest closing Brave (or their other apps) to free RAM. Shrink our own side instead: VM RAM, test processes, context, one job at a time. See [[strata-ram-budget]].
